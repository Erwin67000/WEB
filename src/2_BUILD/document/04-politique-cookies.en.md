# Cookie policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

Complements the [privacy policy](/confidentialite).

The Site sets what is **necessary** to run the service and **remember language** (FR / EN). **Plausible** provides cookieless pageview statistics (no advertising identifier). A **non-mandatory** button on the product page and configurator can also enable **enriched Plausible events** and the **newsletter**. That button is **not required to order**; a second click turns it off. Consent is stored as `philae-consent-extras`.

**Strictly necessary:** Cloudflare security cookies; `philae-lang`; shop panel-colour preference; local configuration. **Google Fonts** is a third-party call (IP may be sent to Google). **Stripe** payment cookies will appear only **when** checkout is enabled; this page will then be updated.

No advertising banner is shown. Blocking third-party cookies may affect typefaces. A script blocker may prevent Plausible from counting visits.

No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel.
