# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

**ATELIER PHILAE** (SASU, 28 rue Kuhn, 67000 Strasbourg — contact@philae.design) uses your data to reply, make, deliver and invoice: identity, contact, address, piece configuration. Online payment is handled by Stripe; we do not keep card numbers.

You can order as a guest or create an account (e-mail link, Google or Apple). The **newsletter** is sent only after you opt in, when creating an account or checking out as a guest. Unsubscribe from My account or by e-mail.

Site traffic is measured with **Plausible** (aggregated stats, no advertising cookies). Your e-mail is not sent to Plausible.

We do not sell your data. It is shared only as needed: workshop, carrier, Cloudflare, Plausible, Stripe. For deliveries in France, the eco-contribution is declared to Ecomaison.

Retention: orders as required by law; newsletter until you unsubscribe.

Your rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection): contact@philae.design. Complaint: [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr).
