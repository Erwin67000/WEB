# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026.

How **ATELIER PHILAE** processes personal data on https://www.philae.design/, under the GDPR and French data-protection law.

**Controller:** ATELIER PHILAE, SASU, 28 rue Kuhn, 67000 Strasbourg, France. Contact: contact@philae.design. No DPO has been appointed.

**Data:** identity and contact; order/configuration; delivery address (including country, to determine French eco-contribution); technical CDN logs (Cloudflare); language preference; optional newsletter (opt-in). **Stripe is not yet enabled**; no card numbers are stored. **Google Fonts** may collect IP addresses.

**Purposes:** contract performance (order, manufacture, delivery); legal duties (accounts, warranties, eco-contribution for France deliveries); security; replies to messages; consent for newsletter.

**Recipients:** ATELIER PHILAE staff; Stripe when online payment is enabled; Cloudflare; the carrier; **Ecomaison** (market-placement declarations for France deliveries, not the full customer file); **CM2C** if mediation is opened; accountant, counsel, authorities if required; Google (fonts).

**Retention:** orders/invoices 5–10 years; unused leads 3 years; newsletter until unsubscribe.

**Rights:** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection. CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr

See also the [cookie policy](/politique-cookies).
